Our Story

Simple food, made with real care.

Sable began with a simple belief — that good food does not need to be complicated. It needs good hands, good ingredients, and a little patience.

Our sourdough starter is fed every day. The dough rests and slow-ferments for days — that is what gives our pizza its light, airy crust and deep flavour. Nothing is rushed, because the best things never are.

Tucked away on 80 Feet Road in Indiranagar, Sable is where friends, family, and good conversation come together over a warm, wood-fired plate.

— The Sable Family
